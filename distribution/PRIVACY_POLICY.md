# Digital Munshi – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

Digital Munshi ("the app") helps you keep a personal ledger, share event
expenses and manage small-business records. This policy explains what data
the app handles and why.

## Data we collect
- **Account details:** your name, email address and (optionally) a profile photo.
- **Records you enter:** ledger transactions, event ledgers and their members,
  business products, sales, expenses and team members.
- **Device push token:** used only to deliver notifications to your devices.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the
app contains no ads.

## How we use it
- To sign you in and keep your records in sync across your devices.
- To share records with people you invite (event members, business staff).
- To send notifications you've opted into (reminders, invites, activity).

## Where it is stored
Data is stored on your device and in Google Firebase (Authentication,
Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Messaging), operated by Google LLC.
Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are handled by Firebase
Authentication and are never stored by the app.

## Sharing
We do not sell or rent your data. Records are visible only to you and the
people you share a ledger or business with.

## Your choices
- Edit or delete your records at any time in the app.
- Turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
- Request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us
  at the address below; we complete deletion within 30 days.

## Children
