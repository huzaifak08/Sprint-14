## Full description (max 4000)
Digital Munshi is your pocket bookkeeper. Track your own money, split costs
with friends and keep your small business accounts in one simple app that
works even without internet.

PERSONAL LEDGER
• Record income and expenses with categories, payment methods and notes
• Find any transaction in seconds: search by words or amounts (e.g. ">1000")
• Filter by date range, category, payment method or amount
• Monthly income vs. expense chart and top spending categories
• Select transactions to add them up instantly

EVENT & GROUP EXPENSES
• Create ledgers for trips, hostels, functions or any shared cost
• Collect a common pool from one person, equally from several, or custom amounts
• Split each expense between exactly the people who shared it
• See who owes whom, with the fewest payments needed to settle up

SMALL BUSINESS
• Manage products, record sales and track expenses
• Invite staff with owner, admin or salesman roles
• Dashboard with sales trends and summaries

BUILT TO BE RELIABLE
• Works offline: everything is saved on your phone first
• Syncs automatically to the cloud when you're back online
• Light, dark or system theme
• Optional app lock with fingerprint, face or device PIN

Digital Munshi keeps your accounts in order, so you don't have to.
